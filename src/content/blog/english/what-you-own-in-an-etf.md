## Read the mandate first

The product label is only the beginning. Its mandate, holdings, and implementation explain what exposure you would be buying.

This foundation piece would walk through an actual factsheet and prospectus, keeping the sources beside the explanation.

## Connect it to the portfolio

Ask which role the exposure would play and what you already own.

The next step is a comparison of overlapping holdings, costs, and the trade-offs relevant to your own plan.
