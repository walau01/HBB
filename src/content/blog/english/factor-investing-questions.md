## What is the proposed explanation?

Start with the reasoning behind the result. Then ask what the test can and cannot establish.

A research piece needs to distinguish the historical observation from the proposed explanation and from a forecast.

## Can the result survive implementation?

Costs, turnover, definitions, and the chosen sample all deserve attention.

This draft frames the questions. A complete article would add primary research and counterevidence before reaching a conclusion.
