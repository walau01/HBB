walau01 documents an amateur investor’s effort to understand personal finance, ETFs, and evidence-based investing.

## What you will find here

- Personal investing stories and lessons
- Explanations of ETFs, diversification, fees, and risk
- Notes about financial research and decision-making
- Transparent calculators and tools as they are developed

## A useful reading order

The first planned articles will explain why I started learning about money, why I chose ETFs, what evidence-based investing means to me, and which investing mistakes I am trying not to repeat.

## Important

I am not a financial professional. The material on this site is general educational information and personal reflection, not personalised financial, tax, or legal advice.
